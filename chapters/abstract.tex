\chapter*{Abstract}
This report presents the development of \textbf{Hubly}, a centralized marketplace platform designed to streamline and professionalize the connection between digital influencers and companies. The primary goal of the project is to resolve the significant communication barriers, fragmented outreach channels, and lack of transparency that currently hinder the negotiation and management of marketing partnerships in the modern creator economy.

Hubly includes key features such as structured profile architectures for granular niche filtering, an advanced search algorithm, robust user authentication with email verification, an integrated in-app messaging system, and a recommendation feed based on user historic preferences. To address the operational complexities of multi-channel outreach, the platform implements a customizable Customer Relationship Management (CRM) pipeline through private conversation tags. This feature allows users to track communication states discreetly, coupled with a data-driven analytics dashboard monitoring key performance indicators like response rates and engagement milestones. Additionally, the platform provides transparency through real-time availability status management and peer-based rating systems driven by authentic user feedback.

The backend of the application is developed using \textbf{ASP.NET Core} and \textbf{Entity Framework Core}, with \textbf{PostgreSQL} as the database engine, ensuring optimized multi-criteria query execution. The system also includes consistent error handling mechanisms and utilizes interceptors to make the authentication process simpler and more organized. The frontend leverages modern web technologies, including \textbf{React} and \textbf{Next.js}, to deliver an intuitive, high-performance web interface optimized for dual-user workflows. The overall architecture follows a clear separation of concerns and applies well-established design patterns to enhance maintainability and security.

Overall, Hubly offers users greater control, efficiency, and insight into their marketing campaigns and professional partnerships by leveraging modern technology to deliver a practical, reliable, and market-ready enterprise solution.
