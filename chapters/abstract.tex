\chapter*{Abstract}
\paragraph{}This report presents the development of \textbf{Hubly}, a centralized marketplace web application designed to streamline and professionalize the connection between digital influencers and companies. The primary goal of the project is to resolve the significant communication barriers, fragmented outreach channels, and lack of transparency that currently hinder the negotiation and management of marketing partnerships in the modern creator economy.

Hubly includes key features such as a structured profile architecture, allowing users to use this app as a Company or a Creator and expose their information that can be used for granular niche filtering, with a search algorithm. Besides that, provides a secure user authentication with email verification, an integrated in-app messaging system (where Creators and Companies can close deals), and a recommendation feed based on users' historical preferences. To address the operational complexity of multi-channel outreach, Hubly implements private conversation tags, allowing users to track communication states discreetly. Since the application focuses on transparency and partnerships, users can also rate each other based on their experiences. Furthermore, Hubly also improves transparency through real-time availability status management.

The backend of the application is developed using \textbf{ASP.NET Core} and \textbf{Entity Framework Core}, with \textbf{PostgreSQL} as the database engine, ensuring optimized multi-criteria query execution. The system also includes consistent error handling mechanisms and utilizes interceptors to make the authentication process simpler and more organized. The frontend leverages modern web technologies, including \textbf{React} and \textbf{Next.js}, to deliver an intuitive, high-performance web interface optimized for dual-user workflows. The overall architecture follows a clear separation of concerns and applies well-established design patterns to enhance maintainability and security.

Overall, Hubly offers users greater control, efficiency, and insight into their marketing campaigns and professional partnerships by leveraging modern technology to deliver a practical, reliable, and market-ready enterprise solution.
