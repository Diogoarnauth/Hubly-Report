\chapter{Introduction}

\paragraph{}This report presents the design and implementation of Hubly, a web application,
developed as part of the course \textit{Project and Seminar}. The project was undertaken by a team
of two students, with the goal of creating a platform that connects influencers with businesses, 
enabling seamless partnerships and collaborations.

\section{Context}

\paragraph{}The motivation for this project emerged from a previous independent web application developed by the authors, focused on providing information about topics related to the financial and crypto markets. During that project, the need to promote the application and increase its visibility led to the search for digital content creators who could communicate with audiences interested in those topics. This process revealed several practical difficulties. Identifying suitable creators required comparing multiple factors, such as the creator’s platform, audience size, content area, availability and commercial conditions. In addition, contacting several creators simultaneously resulted in communication being spread across different channels, including social networks, email, instant messaging applications and online forms. As a consequence, it became difficult to track the context of each conversation, remember the negotiation stage of each contact and maintain an organized view of the overall outreach process. This experience highlighted a problem in influencer partnerships workflows, the absence of a centralized system that supports the discovery, communication and tracking of the stage of the outgoing conversation between companies and digital creators. 
Hubly was therefore proposed as a system designed to address this type of fragmentation. The project focuses on creating a centralized environment where companies and creators can represent their profiles, search for relevant contacts and manage communication in a more structured way.

\section{Solution}
\paragraph{}Hubly is a web application where creators and companies (big and small business) can authenticated freely and centralize the main stages involved in partnerships. The system provides a common environment where users can represent themselves as either companies or creators, search for relevant profiles, initiate conversations and organize the state of potential collaborations.

A central aspect of the solution is the distinction between a creator and the creator’s social profiles. In the context of this project, a creator is not represented only as a single generic profile. Instead, a creator may have multiple platform-specific social profiles, each corresponding to a different social media presence, such as Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and more. This modelling decision is relevant because the same creator may have different audience sizes, content sectors and commercial conditions depending on the platform Figure \ref{fig:solution} illustrates this conceptual structure.

Companies are represented through company profiles containing information relevant to discovery and contact. Creators, on the other hand, can organize their activity through one or more social profiles. Based on these entities, Hubly supports filtered search, allowing companies and creators to discover potential partners according to criteria such as sector, platform, audience size, price range, company size and country.

The solution also includes internal conversations associated with company or creator social profile. This allows communication to remain connected to the specific context of a possible partnership, instead of being distributed across external channels, and in the messaging to support the management of several simultaneous contacts, the system provides private conversation tags, allowing each user to classify conversations according to their own workflow. In addition, Hubly includes profile-view history, creator ratings, availability status, verified accounts, and a recommendation mechanism based on previous user searches. To support collaborative workflows, the system features a team-management module, enabling multiple users from the same organization, such as a marketing team or a professional agency, to interact with the platform under a shared corporate account through an invitation-based access model. Finally, to ensure operational transparency and data integrity, the system incorporates an audit logging mechanism, providing a reliable record of critical operations performed within the organization's account.

\subsection{Typical Use Case}
Hubly can be used in different scenarios involving companies and digital content creators. A possible use case is as follows:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item A creator registers in the system and creates one or more social profiles, each associated with a specific platform, such as Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and more.
    \item A company registers in the system and creates its company profile, including information such as its sectors, company size and country headquarters.
    \item The owner of a company or creator profile may invite coworkers to collaborate within the same profile environment.
    \item After accepting the invitation, coworkers can access the shared workspace according to their assigned role, allowing multiple users to manage communication and partnership-related activities under the same company or creator profile.
    \item The company searches for creator social profiles using filters such as platform, sector, audience size and price range.
    \item The company selects a creator social profile and initiates an internal conversation.
    \item During the negotiation process, each user can assign private tags to the conversation, according to their own workflow.
    \item The system stores profile views, which may later be used to support recommendations.
    \item The system records relevant actions in audit logs, allowing profile owners to track activity performed within the shared workspace.
    \item After an interaction or collaboration, users may rate creators, contributing to the reputation information available in the system.
\end{enumerate}
